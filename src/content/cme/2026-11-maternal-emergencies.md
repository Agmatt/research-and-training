This is a simulation-based session with hands-on drills in our maternity
unit. Participants rotate through three scenarios with structured
debriefing after each.

**Scenarios:**

1. **Postpartum haemorrhage** — recognition, medical management, and
   escalation
2. **Eclampsia** — magnesium sulphate administration, airway management,
   and delivery decision
3. **Neonatal resuscitation** — the golden minute, ventilation, and
   escalation

**Format:** Hands-on simulation with role-play, followed by structured
debriefing. Space is limited to 12 participants to allow meaningful
rotation.
