This session uses real anonymised cases from our emergency department over
the past quarter. Participants work through triage decisions, initial
management, and referral criteria for trauma, obstetric emergencies, and
acute medical presentations.

**What you'll cover:**

- Structured approach to the undifferentiated emergency patient
- Triage decision-making under resource constraints
- Initial management of traumatic injuries
- When to refer — and to where
- Documentation and handover

**Format:** Case-based discussion with real anonymised cases, group
breakout scenarios, and structured debriefing. No simulation equipment
required — this is a discussion-driven workshop.
